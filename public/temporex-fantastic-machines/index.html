Album Marketing Plan

Temporex

Fantastic Machines
Prepared for Independent Co. · $14,000 media budget · Jul–Nov 2026

Temporex comes into this album with about 1.4M Spotify monthly listeners, mostly catalog, and a social footprint a fraction of that size. That gap is a conversion problem, not an awareness one. This is the digital plan that converts it across four singles into Fantastic Machines: paid media behind the strongest asset on every drop, DSP conversion (pre-save, Marquee, Showcase, and an album Countdown that opens at Real Time), and Strive FM rideshare playlisting as the album's streaming hedge. The $14K below is what CCD deploys, drop by drop.

Total media budget$14,000

Marketing only, and separate from production: Ababa's animation retainer (~$6.5K / mo) and the music-video shoots ride the recording / production line, not this $14K. The ~$2,000 reserve is held centrally, released mid-cycle to double down on whichever single overperforms. There is no paid creator / seeding line on this budget: reach comes from paid media amplifying the strongest organic and live content, plus the DSP stack. Activations below are greenlight-later concepts, not costed against this $14K.

Creative activations

Three greenlight-later plays

World-building that captures owned audience. Pitch concepts, not costed against the $14K.

Web game

The Machine Room

A browser-playable room built from the album's Ababa Blender assets. Players parkour through the Fantastic Machines world; in-world billboards read “new single, out now.” A gamified gate (“your phone number is your ticket to the ride”) drops email / phone into Laylo.

Why. Converts curiosity into an owned email / SMS list and reuses assets we're already building. Clip the playthrough and it doubles as the album's dark-ride content.
Custom microsite

Archive: the Machine repair manual

A standalone site framed as a repair manual for a machine that never existed. Each page is one illustrated component with a made-up name; new parts get added across the rollout, and the full machine only reveals on album day.

Why. A collectible artifact fans share, the modern liner notes. Generates inbound press and becomes the destination link from every drop.
IRL booth

Collaborative Animation Booth

A booth at a live activation. Heavy Manners, the LA animation space Joseph teaches at, is a natural fit, alongside adjacent animated-shorts events. Attendees draw one component of the machine on an iPad; each drawing joins a growing master illustration, and by album day the machine is fully assembled, built by strangers.

Why. Turns every event into a content moment and a fan artifact. “Your drawing made it into the film” converts attendees into superfans.
